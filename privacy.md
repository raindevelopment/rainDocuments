# Privacy Policy

**OpenGym** · Last updated: 18 November 2026

OpenGym is built so your fitness, health, and nutrition data stays on your device. We do not run user accounts or servers that store your data, we show no ads, and we do not track you across apps or websites.

## 2. Data stored only on your device

The following is stored locally in the App's private storage on your iPhone or iPad. We do not receive a copy.

- **Profile:** name, birth date, sex, height, weight, activity level, goal, and units, all optional.
- **Training:** your plans, the exercises in them, and the workouts you complete, including their duration, sets, and estimated energy.
- **Flow Mode:** your daily readiness check-in and the effort ratings you give after each set, used only to adapt the current session and show the summary.
- **Nutrition and hydration:** meals, macros, food products you chose, drinks, goals, and reminder settings.
- **Meal photos:** photos you take or pick for meal analysis. They are analyzed on your device with Apple's Vision framework and are never uploaded.
- **Cached exercise images,** so your plans work offline.

This data is included in your device backups (iCloud or computer) if you have backups turned on. Those backups are governed by Apple's privacy policy, not ours.

## 3. Apple Health (HealthKit)

If you allow it, OpenGym reads the categories you approve (for example steps, active energy, exercise minutes, heart rate, body measurements, and workouts) to show your fitness overview, and writes completed workouts, estimated active energy, height, and weight when you choose to sync.

- Health data is processed only on your device to provide these features.
- We never use Health data for advertising, marketing, or data mining, never sell it, and never share it with third parties.
- We never store Health data in iCloud or on any server we control.
- You can change or revoke access at any time in the Health app → Sharing → Apps → OpenGym.

## 4. Data that leaves your device

The App only sends data off your device in these cases:

| When | Sent to | What is sent | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| You search for a food | Open Food Facts (openfoodfacts.org) | Your search text, your IP address, a generic app identifier | To show matching food products |
| Exercise images are loaded | The image host (currently GitHub, raw.githubusercontent.com, or our content delivery network) | Your IP address and the requested image address | To display exercise photos |
| You buy, restore, or check OpenGym Pro | Apple (App Store) | Handled by Apple under Apple's privacy policy | To process payments and confirm your subscription |
| You open an external link (for example StrengthLog or a study) | That website | Whatever the website collects | Only when you choose to open it |

Your IP address is processed by these hosts to deliver the content. We do not receive or store it. The legal basis is the performance of our contract with you (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR), and our legitimate interest in providing a working app (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

## 5. Purchases

Subscriptions are processed entirely by Apple. We never see your name, email, or payment details. The App receives a cryptographically signed transaction record from Apple on your device (product, purchase date, expiry, and an anonymous transaction ID) to unlock Pro features. We may receive anonymized, aggregated sales reports from Apple.

## 6. What we do not do

- No advertising, and no advertising SDKs.
- No analytics or crash-reporting SDKs that collect personal data.
- No tracking across apps or websites, and no data brokers.
- No sale or sharing of personal data.
- No user accounts.

If we add an analytics or crash-reporting service in the future, we will update this policy, ask for consent where the law requires it, and update the App Store privacy label.

## 7. Notifications and permissions

Hydration reminders are scheduled locally on your device and are not sent through a server. The camera, photo library, notification, and Health permissions are requested only when you use the related feature, and you can change them at any time in iOS Settings.

## 8. How long we keep data

Because your data is stored on your device, you control how long it is kept. You can delete individual entries in the App, or remove all App data by deleting the App. Data written to Apple Health stays in Health until you delete it there.

## 9. Your rights

Under the GDPR and similar laws you have the right to access, correct, delete, restrict, and port your personal data, to object to processing, and to withdraw consent at any time. Because we do not hold your data on our systems, you can exercise most of these rights directly in the App or in iOS Settings. For any question or request, contact us at nikomokrov@gmail.com. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority, in particular in your country of residence.

California and other U.S. state residents: we do not sell or share personal information, and we do not use sensitive personal information to infer characteristics about you.

## 10. Children

The App is not directed to children under 16, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from them.

## 11. Security

Data on your device is protected by iOS security features, including app sandboxing and data protection encryption when your device is locked with a passcode. All network requests made by the App use encrypted HTTPS connections.

We will update this policy when the App changes how it handles data. The date at the top shows the latest version. We will tell you about material changes in the App.
